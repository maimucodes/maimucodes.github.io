research

Multimodal depression detection

Undergraduate thesis, using deep learning

BRAC University, Dept. of CSE · Jan 2025 to June 2026
Supervisor: Md. Tawhid Anwar, Senior Lecturer, CSE

I designed and deployed a multimodal deep learning pipeline that detects depression from facial and acoustic signals. It covers the full machine learning workflow, from data preprocessing and feature extraction to ensemble optimization.

I translated the models' outputs into evaluation metrics (AUC, recall and accuracy) so the research conclusions could be read and checked.

  1. InputFacial and acoustic signals
  2. PrepareData preprocessing
  3. Features273-dimensional feature extraction
  4. ModelEnsemble of RF, MLP and CatBoost
  5. EvaluateAUC, recall and accuracy

  • 76.92%

    Depression detection accuracy, ahead of published baseline models

  • 93.24%

    Accuracy of the FER ensemble model

  • 72.47%

    Accuracy of both models combined, on a test set of 149 videos with audio
